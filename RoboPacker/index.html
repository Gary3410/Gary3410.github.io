RoboPacker: An Autonomous Robotic Packing System for General Objects

In Submit

Anonymous submission



Abstract

In this paper, we propose an autonomous robot packing system designed to tightly store cluttered general objects into shipping boxes with high space utilization, thereby significantly reducing the labor cost of object packaging. Efficient compact packing in confined industrial spaces is labor-intensive when done manually. While robots can perform pre-programmed pick-and-place tasks, developing autonomous, versatile packing robots is hindered by object variability, occlusion, and tight packing environments. Specifically, we first devise an open-vocabulary shape estimation method to reconstruct the complete point cloud for cluttered objects from both seen and novel categories, and we also generate effective interactions to the object clutter to collect informative visual clue for shape estimation in high uncertainty. Moreover, we introduce a hierarchical reinforcement learning framework to plan the order, location and orientation for object package with the goal of achieving the maximal space utilization ratio. We build the robotic packing system by integrating the presented techniques with feasible manipulation techniques for realistic implementation. In real-world experiments, our RoboPacker can tightly pack 20 densely cluttered everyday objects from 8 seen and 4 novel classes into the 40$\times$40$\times$20 cm shipping box with 0.355 space utilization ratio and 73.3% success rate.

Approach

The pipeline of RoboPacker. Compositions of our robotic packing system including open-vocabulary shape estimation, interactive object information collection and hierarchical reinforcement learning based packing planning. Feasible manipulation techniques containing grasping, placement and rearrangement are integrated with the presented approaches to build the complete system.
